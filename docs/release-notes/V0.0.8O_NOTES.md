# v0.0.8o notes

## Covered now
LimitSearch, ToneOut, Waterfall, CustomWfBand, WfColors, BandDefault, PresetBroadcastScreen, CustomBroadcastScreen (plus everything from v0.0.8k-n).

## Where your real file differs from the specification
- CustomWfBand rows have 9 fields (specification: 7); the last two are empty and preserved.
- Waterfall and CustomWfBand names are shown even though empty in your file.
- IfxFreqs holds two empty fields (specification: 17). It stays locked because I have no real populated example.

## My own limits (not in the specification)
- Names for Limit Search, Tone-Out, Waterfall and its bands are limited to 16 printable ASCII characters. Your real names (for example `WF Custom Band 0`) are exactly 16 characters, which suggests that is the scanner's limit. If Sentinel allows longer names, tell me and I will raise it.
- Frequencies must be whole Hz between 25,000,000 and 1,300,000,000; the scanner's coverage gaps are not enforced.

## Not covered
DispOptItems and DispColors (display layouts and colours), IfxFreqs, AvoidFreqs.
