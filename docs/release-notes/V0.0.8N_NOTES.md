# v0.0.8n notes

## Covered now (profile_schema.py)
DisplayOption, Backlight, Battery, OwnerInfo, ClockOption, RecordingOption, StandbyOption, added to GlobalSetting, SearchCommon, CloseCall, Weather and WxSameList.

## Where your real file differs from the specification
- DisplayOption: 13 fields; Simple mode, EDACS TGID format and colour mode are positions 11-13 (specification: 12-14).
- Backlight: 11 fields; brightness is position 6 and the SDS200 dimmer fields do not exist. Positions 2 and 5 hold values (`High`, `30`) in the specification's "reserved" slots; they are preserved and not editable.
- ClockOption position 1 holds `24H` (specification: reserved). Read-only until a profile with `12H` confirms the spelling; send one if you want it editable.
- StandbyOption is not in the specification at all; shown read-only.

## Rules
Choice lists come from the specification (time zone -14.0 to 14.0 in 0.5 steps, replay duration 0 or 30-240 in 30s, brightness 10-100 in 10s, etc.). Owner info lines accept printable ASCII up to 24 characters and may be empty.
